How to Spot and Stop Bad Habits in Your Child

Before They Carry Them Into Adulthood

By Dr. Tope Adeniyi

Every parent has watched a small behavior repeat itself one too many times and wondered: Is this just a phase, or is this becoming who they are?

The truth is, the habits your child practises today — the ones that seem small, ordinary, even forgettable — are quietly building the adult they will become. And the window to shape those habits is shorter than most parents realise.

In this practical, compassionate guide, child psychologist and parenting coach Dr. Tope Adeniyi draws on over 25 years of working directly with children and families to help you:

  • Spot the early warning signs of a forming habit before it takes root
  • Understand the real reasons children develop difficult behaviors — from lying and aggression to procrastination and screen dependency
  • Respond with strategies that actually work, without fear, shame, or guesswork
  • Replace harmful habits with positive ones that last well into adulthood

Covering 12 essential areas — including dishonesty, anger, laziness, bullying, screen addiction, and emotional intelligence — this book gives you a clear, step-by-step framework for every stage from toddlerhood to the teenage years.

Each chapter includes real-life parenting scenarios, practical strategies you can start today, common mistakes to avoid, and a quiz to help you apply what you've learned.

Whether your child is 2 or 18, it is never too early — and never too late — to make a difference.
